## Welcome

Welcome to the OWASP
[Vancouver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver) chapter home page.
We are located in the beautiful province of [British
Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Columbia), on the West
Coast of [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada).

Our mission is to enrich Vancouver's application security community. We
hope you can join us in accomplishing that.

### Upcoming meetings on Meetup

Check out our [meetings on Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/owasp-vancouver-chapter) and come along to learn and connect with other local security folk - we'd love to meet you!

### Share your knowledge as a Speaker

We're always looking for speakers for our meetings, so whethe you have a 5 minute or hour-long talk, let us know by sharing the details via our Speaker form: [https://forms.gle/thuaQHMzxumG6Wxy6](https://forms.gle/thuaQHMzxumG6Wxy6)

### Join our Slack Channel

Join the conversation in our [#owasp-vancouver](https://mars-comm.slack.com/archives/C6QHGPUAK) channel on the MARS Community Slack Workspace! If you're not already there, [click here to join](https://join.slack.com/t/mars-comm/shared_invite/zt-2e5n00d34-q9tsNfKS_XrtEDWFpRTxKQ).

### Connect with our LinkedIn Group

Learn about our upcoming meetings and other news in our LinkedIn Group: [https://www.linkedin.com/groups/14485841/](https://www.linkedin.com/groups/14485841/)
